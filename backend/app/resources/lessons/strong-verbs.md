# Strong verbs
> "Is able to", "has an effect on", "does a check of": weak verb + noun. A single strong verb is clearer.

## Why it matters
The verb carries the action of a sentence. When it's "is", "has" or "does", the real action hides in a noun and the sentence grows longer.

## How to fix it
- "is able to" → "can"
- "has an effect on" → "affects"
- "does a check of" → "checks"
- "gives an indication of" → "indicates"
- "there is a need to" → "we must"
- "There are three sensors that measure …" → "Three sensors measure …"

## Example
**Before:** There is a relay that has the function of switching the pump, and it is able to handle 10 A.

**After:** A relay switches the pump and handles up to 10 A.

## Try it
Circle every "is", "has" and "does" in one paragraph. Replace two of them with the action itself.
