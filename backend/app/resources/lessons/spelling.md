# Spelling
> Typos cost credibility, especially in technical terms and units.

## What to watch
- **Technical terms:** "resistence" → "resistance", "capacitence" → "capacitance", "guage" → "gauge".
- **British vs American:** pick one and keep it ("aluminium/aluminum", "analyse/analyze", "metre/meter" for length). Follow your department's style.
- **Names of standards and tools:** copy them exactly (IS 1498, MATLAB, LabVIEW).
- **Homophones:** "effect/affect", "principal/principle", "discreet/discrete".

## How OwnIt handles it
Abbreviations, names, code, equations and numbers are protected, so the spell check won't flag "ESP32" or "kN". Everything else goes through LanguageTool.
