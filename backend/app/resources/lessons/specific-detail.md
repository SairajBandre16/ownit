# Specific detail: making it yours
> Generic writing could be about anyone's project. Specific writing could only be about yours.

## What makes text generic
- vague amounts: "various sensors", "a wide range of applications";
- claims without examples: "IoT plays a crucial role in agriculture";
- results without numbers: "the efficiency improved";
- a conclusion with no view of your own.

## How to make it specific
- **Name things:** the board (ESP32), the sensor model, the lab, the standard (IS 456).
- **Give numbers with units:** what you measured, how often, how much it changed.
- **Add one example from your own work** for each general claim.
- **Say what you learned** or would change: examiners love an honest limitation.

## Example
**Before:** IoT is widely used in agriculture and has many benefits.

**After:** On our 1-acre test plot, the ESP32 controller watered only when the soil moisture fell below 30%, which cut water use from 120 L to 84 L a week.
