# Figures and tables
> Every figure and table is numbered in the order it is first mentioned, has a caption, and is referred to in the text.

## The rules
- **Refer to each one in the text** before or near where it appears: "The output rose steadily (Fig. 2)."
- **Number in order of first mention:** the first figure you mention is Figure 1.
- **Caption every one.** Figure captions go below the figure; table captions go above the table.
- **Make captions self-contained:** "Figure 3: Output power of fixed and tracking panels over one day (12 March)."
- **Say what to notice**, don't just point: "Fig. 2 shows that the output peaks at noon" beats "See Fig. 2."

## Example
**Before:** The results are shown below. [graph with no caption]

**After:** Fig. 2 shows that the tracking panel produced more energy from 9:00 to 16:00.
*Figure 2: Hourly energy of the fixed and tracking panels on 12 March.*

## Try it
List your figures in the order you first mention them. If the list isn't 1, 2, 3 …, renumber.
