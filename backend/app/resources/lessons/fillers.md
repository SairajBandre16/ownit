# Fillers
> "Very", "really", "basically", "quite", "actually": words that sound like emphasis but add nothing.

## Why it matters
Fillers weaken the words around them. "Very important" is weaker than "essential", and "quite high" leaves the reader guessing. In technical writing a number beats an intensifier every time.

## How to fix it
- Delete the filler and read the sentence again. Usually it's stronger.
- Replace "very + adjective" with a precise word: "very small" → "tiny", "very fast" → "rapid".
- Replace vague size words with the value: "quite high temperature" → "a temperature of 85 °C".

## Example
**Before:** The results are basically very good and the error is really quite small.

**After:** The error was 0.4%, well inside the 2% limit.

## Try it
Delete every "very" and "really" in one paragraph. Put a number where the sentence now feels empty.
