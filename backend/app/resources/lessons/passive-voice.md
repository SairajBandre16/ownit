# Passive voice
> "The data was recorded by the logger" (passive) vs "The logger recorded the data" (active).

## When passive is fine
In a Methodology section, the passive is conventional: "The specimen was loaded at 2 mm/min." The reader cares about the procedure, not who pressed the button. OwnIt doesn't suggest active voice there.

## When to prefer active
In the Introduction, Results and Discussion, active sentences are shorter and name the cause:

- "The temperature was increased by the heater" → "The heater increased the temperature."
- "It was found that the output was improved by tracking" → "Tracking improved the output."

## How to spot it
A form of "be" (is, was, were, been) + a past participle (measured, used, recorded), often followed by "by …".

## Example
**Before:** A significant reduction in vibration was observed after the damper was installed by the team.

**After:** After we installed the damper, the vibration fell by 60%.

## Try it
Find a passive sentence with "by". Make the thing after "by" the subject.
