# Stock phrases
> "Delve into", "plays a crucial role", "in today's fast-paced world", "a myriad of": phrases common in generated drafts.

## Why it matters
These phrases are grammatical but empty. They make a report sound generic, and a reader who has seen hundreds of them stops trusting the text. They also make it hard for you to explain the sentence in your own words.

## How to fix it
- "delve into" → "examine", "study"
- "plays a crucial role in" → say what it does: "controls", "limits", "sets"
- "in today's fast-paced world" → delete it
- "a myriad of" / "a plethora of" → "many", or the number
- "harness the power of" → "use"
- "pave the way for" → "make possible", or name what it enables

## Example
**Before:** In today's fast-paced world, IoT plays a crucial role in agriculture and paves the way for a myriad of applications.

**After:** IoT sensors let a farmer water only when the soil is dry. Our controller used one to cut water use by 30%.

## Try it
Rewrite one flagged sentence so it contains a name, a number or a part from your own project.
