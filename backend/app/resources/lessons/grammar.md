# Grammar
> The grammar check comes from LanguageTool, an open-source rule-based checker running on the OwnIt server.

## Common problems in reports
- **Subject–verb agreement:** "The results of the test shows" → "The results … show". Find the real subject.
- **Articles:** "We used Arduino board" → "We used an Arduino board". Countable nouns in the singular need "a/an/the".
- **Tense switching inside a sentence:** "We measured the voltage and record it" → "measured … and recorded".
- **Run-on sentences:** two sentences joined by a comma. Use a full stop or a joining word.

## How to use the suggestions
Each grammar issue shows what the checker found and, often, a fix. Read the sentence with the fix before you accept it: automatic suggestions can miss technical meaning.

## Example
**Before:** The data from the sensors are send to the cloud, it is displayed on a dashboard.

**After:** The data from the sensors are sent to the cloud and displayed on a dashboard.

## Try it
Read your Results section aloud slowly. Your ear catches agreement errors that your eyes skip.
