# Units and numbers
> A number without its unit is not a result. Write units the SI way.

## The rules
- **A space between number and unit:** "12 V", "25 kN", "60 °C" (not "12V"). Exceptions: "91%" and angles "45°".
- **Case matters:** "kg" not "KG", "kW" not "kw", "mA" not "MA" (MA is mega-ampere).
- **No plural for symbols:** "5 kg" not "5 kgs", "2 h" not "2 hrs".
- **One system:** don't mix SI and imperial for the same quantity (metres and feet). Convert and state the original in brackets if needed.
- **Every measured value in Results has a unit**, or a word saying what it counts ("3 samples").
- **Significant figures:** report what your instrument can resolve. "2.3456 V" from a two-digit meter is invented precision.

## Example
**Before:** The motor drew 2A at 12v and the shaft turned at 1500 RPMs; the frame was 3 ft long.

**After:** The motor drew 2 A at 12 V and the shaft turned at 1500 rpm; the frame was 0.9 m long.
