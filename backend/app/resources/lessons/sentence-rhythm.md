# Sentence rhythm
> Text where every sentence has the same length reads like a list. Mix short and long sentences.

## Why it matters
Drafts produced quickly (and by AI tools) often have sentences of almost identical length and shape. Readers lose attention in that flat rhythm, and the important point doesn't stand out. Variety tells the reader where to slow down.

## How to fix it
- After two or three long sentences, add a short one that states the key point.
- Join two short sentences about the same thing with "and", "so" or a semicolon.
- Put your most important finding in the shortest sentence of the paragraph.

## Example
**Before:** The tracker was built from aluminium sections. The motors were mounted on the frame. The sensors were placed on each side. The controller read the sensors every second.

**After:** The tracker was built from aluminium sections, with a motor on each axis and a light sensor on each side. Every second, the controller read the sensors. That was fast enough.
