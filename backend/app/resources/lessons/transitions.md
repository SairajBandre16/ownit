# Transitions
> "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally" at the start of every sentence: the links become noise.

## Why it matters
A transition should tell the reader how two ideas relate: addition, contrast, cause, result or example. If every sentence starts with one, none of them stands out, and repeated "Moreover" is a strong sign of a generated draft.

## How to fix it
- Use a transition only when the link isn't obvious from the content.
- Choose the one that names the relation: "However" (contrast), "So" / "As a result" (result), "For example" (example), "Because" (cause).
- Vary them, and let most sentences start without one.

## Example
**Before:** Moreover, the sensor is cheap. Furthermore, it is accurate. Additionally, it uses little power. Moreover, it is easy to wire.

**After:** The sensor is cheap and accurate, uses little power and takes four wires to connect. Its only drawback is drift above 50 °C.

## Try it
Delete every "Moreover" and "Furthermore" in a paragraph. Add back only the links the reader needs.
