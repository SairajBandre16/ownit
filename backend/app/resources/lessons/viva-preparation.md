# Preparing for a viva
> A viva checks that you understand what you handed in. Practise answering out loud, not re-reading.

## What examiners ask
Questions usually climb a ladder:
1. **Define:** "What is a PLC?" "What does MOSFET stand for?"
2. **How:** "How did you calibrate the sensor?" "How does the load affect the output?"
3. **Why:** "Why did you choose a relay instead of a MOSFET?" "Why was your COP lower than the theory?"
4. **Compare and evaluate:** "What would you change if you did it again?" "What are the limitations?"

## How to prepare
- Run the Viva Simulator in step 4 until you reach level 3 or 4 on most concepts.
- For every number in your Results, know where it came from and what it means.
- For every design choice, know one alternative and why you didn't use it.
- Prepare one honest limitation and one improvement.
- Use the Revision Deck daily for a week before the viva: short, spaced sessions beat one long night.

## In the room
- It's fine to pause and think. Say "Let me think about that."
- If you don't know, say what you do know and how you would find out.
- Draw a sketch if it helps; many examiners like it.
