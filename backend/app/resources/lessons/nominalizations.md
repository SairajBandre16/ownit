# Nominalizations
> "Make an adjustment", "perform a measurement", "give consideration": the action is hidden in a noun. Use the verb.

## Why it matters
Nouns made from verbs (-tion, -ment, -ance) paired with weak verbs (make, give, perform, carry out) make sentences longer and vaguer. The verb version says who does what.

## How to fix it
Find the -tion/-ment noun and turn it back into a verb:

- "perform a calibration of the sensor" → "calibrate the sensor"
- "make an improvement to the design" → "improve the design"
- "carry out an investigation into" → "investigate"
- "the reduction of losses was achieved" → "we reduced the losses"

## Example
**Before:** The implementation of the filter led to a reduction in noise and an improvement in the accuracy of the measurement.

**After:** The filter reduced the noise and made the measurement more accurate.

## Try it
Search your text for "-tion of" and "-ment of". Each is a chance to use a verb.
