# Report structure
> Lab and project reports follow a standard order so examiners can find things fast.

## The usual sections
1. **Abstract:** the whole report in under 250 words: aim, method, key result with numbers, conclusion.
2. **Introduction:** the problem and why it matters.
3. **Objectives:** what you set out to do, as a short list.
4. **Theory / Background:** the principles and prior work you rely on, with citations.
5. **Methodology:** what you built or did, in enough detail to repeat it.
6. **Results:** what you measured, with figures, tables and units, without interpretation.
7. **Discussion:** what the results mean, errors, and comparison with theory or other work.
8. **Conclusion:** answers to the objectives, limitations, future work.
9. **References:** every source you cited, in one consistent style.

## Common problems
- Results and Discussion mixed into one paragraph (a combined "Results and Discussion" section is fine if it's labelled).
- A Conclusion that repeats the Introduction instead of answering the objectives.
- An abstract without a single number.

## Try it
Write one sentence per section summarising it. If you can't, that section needs work before your viva.
